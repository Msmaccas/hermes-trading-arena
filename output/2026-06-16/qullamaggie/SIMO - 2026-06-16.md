## Phase 3: The ORB (Opening Range Breakout) Entry

Once I'm in on the day-after-gap-open, I watch for an **ORB** — Opening Range Breakout. The high of the first 15 minutes of trading becomes a key level:

- If SIMO breaks above the ORB high on volume within the first 30-60 minutes, I add to the position
- I set my first stop just below the ORB low
- This gives me a very tight, well-defined risk level

The ORB is a confirmation tool. If the stock is strong enough to break above its opening range on follow-through volume, the institutional buying is continuing. If it fails to break the ORB, the stock might be running out of momentum, and I'm more cautious.
