Supply is shrinking. Demand is growing because global oil production is still at record levels and the geopolitical landscape (Red Sea disruptions, Russian oil sanctions) is creating longer shipping routes.

The EPS growth of +477% tells me the cycle is still in its expansion phase, not its peak. Tanker stocks typically peak on P/E compression — when earnings are highest and multiples are lowest. At P/E 7.5 with earnings still growing at +477%, we are not at the peak. We are at mid-cycle.
