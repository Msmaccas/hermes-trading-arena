And what I see in CRDO right now is a stock that's screaming "I'm a market leader in the making."

First, let's talk about the chart. The stock is trading at $250.81, which is well above its 50-day moving average of $213 and miles above its 200-day of $132. That's a textbook setup for a stock that's in a powerful uptrend. The 50-day MA is acting as a solid floor, and the 200-day is so far below that it's almost irrelevant for short-term momentum. When a stock can stay above its 50-day for an extended period, especially after a strong breakout, that's a sign of institutional accumulation. And institutions are what drive these massive moves.

It's not some retail pump-and-dump. This is big money accumulating shares. As I've said before, "When the market has been moving up on falling volume, it's a warning sign. The same holds true when stocks fall on rising volume which usually means that the number of sellers is increasing." Here, we're seeing the opposite: price is rising on rising volume. That's the kind of confirmation I look for. This isn't a dead cat bounce. This is a live cat with a rocket strapped to its back.

The RSI at 62.4 is also telling. It's not overbought, which means there's plenty of room for this stock to run higher before it gets extended. I've seen too many traders sell too early because they think a stock is "overbought" at 70. That's nonsense. In a strong uptrend, stocks can stay overbought for weeks or months. An RSI of 62 is actually a sweet spot—it shows momentum without exhaustion. The stock is still in the early to middle stages of its move, not the blow-off top.

Now, let's talk about the fundamentals that actually matter for a momentum trader. EPS growth of 362% year-over-year? That's not just good—that's exceptional. When I was trading Google in 2004-6, I saw similar explosive earnings growth. Those were the stocks that made me a lot of money. The P/E of 137 might scare off value investors, but I don't care about that. What I care about is the trajectory. Earnings are growing faster than the stock price, which means the P/E is actually contracting on a forward basis. That's a bullish sign.

The sector matters too. Tech is where the leaders are born. And CRDO is in the semiconductor space, which is the backbone of the AI revolution. When I look at the market leaders of the 2009-13 rally, I saw financials like Goldman Sachs and JP Morgan doing well thanks to the Fed's quantitative easing. Today, the Fed is still accommodative, but the leaders are in AI and semiconductors. CRDO is perfectly positioned in that narrative.

Let's also consider the chart pattern. I see a rounded bottom formation that started forming in late 2023 and early 2024. The stock broke out of that base on heavy volume and has been consolidating near its highs. That's a classic continuation pattern. As I wrote in my TradersLog interview, "Apple exhibited a nice rounded bottom and started leading the market higher in 2008, well before the rest of the market followed. Those who recognized this... made a lot of money." CRDO is showing the same kind of leadership. It's not following the market; it's leading it.

And if it holds above that level, the next leg up could take it to $300 or beyond. The volume confirmation tells me the institutions are still buying.

One more thing: the market cap of $63 billion is large enough for liquidity but small enough for explosive growth. This isn't a micro-cap penny stock. It's a legitimate company with real earnings. But it's not so large that it's already priced for perfection. There's still room to run.

In conclusion, CRDO has all the hallmarks of a market leader: a strong uptrend, rising volume, explosive earnings growth, and a chart pattern that's setting up for a continuation move. The RSI is not overbought, the moving averages are aligned bullishly, and the sector tailwind is powerful.
