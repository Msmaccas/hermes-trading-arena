An RSI around 48-50 in the context of a stock that's above both key moving averages suggests the stock is consolidating or basing — not breaking down. This is often where you find those lower-in-the-base entries I've talked about.

> "They're not often trading right at that base pivot. They're looking for lower risk entries, kind of lower within the base, where they can manage risk a lot tighter and put on a larger size."

A company with $200M market cap in the tech sector that's seen its earnings collapse by 84% — the market isn't buying this stock for what it did last quarter. The market is buying it for what it expects to happen next. The question is: is that expectation realistic?

## Position Sizing: The Real Edge
