## 4. Risk Assessment

Let me be honest with you. I don't like this setup. I see too many red flags.

That's the biggest risk. At 108 times earnings, the stock has no margin of safety. If earnings disappoint by even 10%, the stock could drop 30-50%. There's no room for error.

*"The key to making money in stocks is not to get scared out of them."*

But with a P/E of 108, you're going to get scared out of them. Every time the stock drops 10%, you'll wonder if it's the beginning of the end.

**Risk #2: The growth rate is unsustainable.**

362% earnings growth is not sustainable. It's a one-time event. The question is: what's the normalized growth rate? If it's 30%, the PEG is 3.6. That's terrible. If it's 20%, the PEG is 5.4. That's even worse.

**Risk #3: The technical indicators are flashing sell signals.**

The MACD has crossed below the signal line. The Williams %R is at -4.10. The stock is above the upper Bollinger Band. The CCI is at 150. The stochastic is overbought. Every single indicator is saying "sell."

**Risk #4: The beta is 3.2.**

This stock is three times more volatile than the market. If the market drops 10%, this stock could drop 30%. Are you prepared for that?

**Risk #5: I don't understand the business.**

I've said it a thousand times: *"Never invest in any idea you can't illustrate with a crayon."*

I can't illustrate CRDO with a crayon. I don't know what they do. I don't know if their technology is proprietary. I don't know if they have competition. I don't know if their customers are loyal. I don't know anything.

Semiconductors are cyclical. They go up and down with the economy. When the economy slows down, companies stop buying chips. That's when earnings collapse. And with a P/E of 108, a collapse in earnings would be devastating.

**Risk #7: The stock is at an all-time high.**

Buying a stock at an all-time high is risky. You have no margin of safety. You're buying at the peak. Sure, it could go higher. But it could also go lower. And the downside risk is much greater than the upside potential.
