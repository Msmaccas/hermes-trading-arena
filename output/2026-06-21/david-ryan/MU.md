## 4. Risk Assessment — What Could Go Wrong?

Let me be brutally honest with you. I’m a three-time champion. I’ve made money in every market environment. But I’ve also taken my share of losses. And I know how to manage risk.

The biggest risk is that the 770% EPS growth rate starts to decelerate. If next quarter’s earnings come in at 300% instead of 770%, the stock will drop. If they come in at 100%, it will get cut in half.

*"When earnings accelerate from $0.10 to $0.20 to $0.40, you have something. When they go from $0.40 to $0.30, you have a problem."* (MarketWise Interview, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=83J6JYXZ7Wg)

But for MU at these levels, I would start with a **half position** — 5-7% of portfolio. Not a full position. The risk-reward is not good enough for a full position.
