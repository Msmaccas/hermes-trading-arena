## Risk Assessment

Let me be direct about the risks here, because "95% of traders lose money. That's a fact." And the biggest reason traders lose money is that they don't manage risk properly.

**Risk #1: Valuation Risk**
The P/E of 108.73 is extreme. If earnings growth decelerates, the multiple could compress significantly. A stock trading at 100x earnings that drops to 50x earnings would lose 50% of its value even if earnings stay flat. That's the risk of high-multiple stocks.

**Risk #2: Extended Price**
The stock is 12% above its 10-week SMA and 26% above its 30-week SMA. In my 11 years of trading, I've learned that buying extended stocks is a recipe for disaster. "There's nothing worse than you know you could have bought the S&P 500, never looked at the market again, and been up 15%. But instead I dedicated all of my time to the market, took over 600 trades, and not only did I not do 15%, but I lost money." Don't chase extended moves.

**Risk #3: High Beta**
With a beta of 3.229, CRDO will amplify any market selloff. If the S&P drops 5%, CRDO could drop 15-20%. That's a lot of pain if you're not prepared for it.

**Risk #4: Overbought Oscillators**
The stochastic (%K at 95.90), Williams %R at -4.10, and CCI at 150.04 are all flashing overbought signals. While these can stay overbought in strong trends, they increase the probability of a pullback.

**Risk #5: Weak Trend Strength**
The ADX of 18.05 suggests the trend is not strong. This could mean the stock is topping or consolidating. If it's topping, the risk of a significant decline is high.

**Risk #6: Lack of Controlled Base**
I haven't seen a clear controlled base on the weekly chart. "Multi-week bases that are tight and picture perfect near highs — you really want to pay attention." If CRDO hasn't built a proper base, the breakout may not be sustainable.
