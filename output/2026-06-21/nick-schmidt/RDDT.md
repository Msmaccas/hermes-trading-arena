## 4. Risk Assessment

Let me talk about what could go wrong. This is the most important part of my analysis. I’ve learned that the market works with time. The more time you have in your favor, the more advantage you have. But if you get the risk assessment wrong, time works against you.

### Risk 3: High Beta

The beta of 1.912 means the stock is highly correlated with the market but with more volatility. If the market sells off, RDDT could sell off 2-3 times as much. In a bear market, this stock could easily drop 50% or more.

### Risk 4: Lack of a Tight Base

A multi-week bases that are tight and picture perfect near highs — you really want to pay attention. RDDT is not near its highs, and the base is not tight. The volatility is high (ATR of $12.33), and the ADX is low (17.49). This suggests that the stock is still in a consolidation phase, and it could break either way.

### Risk 5: The 30-Week SMA Test

If the stock pulls back to the 30-week SMA at $164.13 and fails to hold, that would be a major bearish signal. It would suggest that the stock is breaking down and could head lower. I would need to see a strong bounce at that level before I can consider buying.

I need to see that the stock is being accumulated, not distributed. The minus_DI is higher than the plus_DI, which suggests selling pressure. The volume is average, which doesn’t indicate strong buying. If I see heavy volume on down days, that would be a red flag.

### Risk 7: Time Decay

The market works with time. If the stock stays in this range for too long, the base could become a distribution pattern rather than an accumulation pattern. The longer it takes to break out, the more likely it is to fail.
