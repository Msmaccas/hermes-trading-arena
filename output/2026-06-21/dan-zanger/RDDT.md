**Risk #2: Low Volume.**
The volume ratio of 1.05 on a 5.43% up day is a red flag. This move is not being confirmed by institutional buying. It could be a dead cat bounce.

**Risk #3: The ADX.**
An ADX of 17.49 means there is no trend. Trading in a non-trending market is a recipe for whipsaws. You buy, it drops. You sell, it rallies. This is how you get chopped up.

**Risk #4: High Beta.**
Beta of 1.912 means if the S&P 500 drops 2%, RDDT could drop 3.8%. This stock is leveraged to the market. If the overall market turns down, RDDT will get crushed.

**Risk #5: The Fed.**
As I said in my interview: "What the Fed is doing has become more important than ever... It's been stepping on the fiscal gas pedal since 2008 and the market has become more focused on the Fed than anything else because stocks have been a big beneficiary of these programs." — Dan Zanger, TradersLog interview, Key #5 https://www.traderslog.com/market-reversals

If the Fed signals tighter policy, high-growth stocks like RDDT will be the first to fall. The P/E of 49.99 makes it vulnerable to rising interest rates.

**Risk #6: Sector Rotation.**
I noted: "At the very early stages of the recovery in 2002, home builders and consumer stocks like department stores and restaurants did well. Financials and semiconductor stocks followed. Next are the energy and commodity stocks such as industrial metals as the economy gets going." — Dan Zanger, TradersLog interview, Key #6 https://www.traderslog.com/market-reversals

RDDT is in Communication Services. This sector is not a leader right now. If money is flowing into energy or financials, RDDT will underperform.

**Risk #7: The "Death Cross."**
The `MA50_above_MA200` is 0. The 50-day MA is below the 200-day MA. This is a long-term bearish signal. It often takes months to reverse.
