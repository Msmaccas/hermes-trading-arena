## 1. Methodology Overview — Episodic Pivot

Kristjan Qullamaggie's Episodic Pivot (EP) strategy identifies stocks that have experienced a massive, explosive breakout (the "episodic" move) followed by a tight, low-volume pullback that represents a low-risk continuation entry. Qullamaggie is one of the most successful momentum traders of the post-2008 era, having turned €12,000 into millions through concentrated EP trading.

Key EP criteria:
1. **Huge prior move:** The stock must have rallied 100–1,000%+ from its lows
2. **Tight pullback:** Price pulls back with very narrow daily ranges (usually 1–3 days of tight action)
3. **Declining volume:** Volume must dry up significantly during the pullback
4. **RS line strength:** The relative strength line must be near highs or confirming the pattern
5. **Catalyst:** Strong fundamental or thematic catalyst driving institutional accumulation

Qullamaggie's own words:

> *"I look for stocks that have had a huge move, then pull back with very tight daily ranges and declining volume."* — Kristjan Qullamaggie, *Chat With Traders* ([source](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=44Uw8JmBcoI))
