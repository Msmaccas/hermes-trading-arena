## 2. Business Overview

HD Hyundai Heavy Industries (formerly Hyundai Heavy Industries) is the world's largest shipbuilding company and a leading defense contractor. The company operates in three primary segments:

- **Shipbuilding:** Commercial vessels — LNG carriers, container ships, tankers, bulk carriers
- **Defense & Naval:** Warships, submarines, naval vessels — serving the Republic of Korea Navy and export customers
- **Offshore & Engineering:** Offshore platforms, floating production facilities, industrial plants

The company is a direct beneficiary of the **global defense super-cycle** driven by:
- Rising geopolitical tensions in East Asia (China, Taiwan, North Korea)
- NATO defense spending commitments (2%+ GDP targets)
- Indo-Pacific naval modernization programs
- South Korea's export-driven defense industry (K-defense)

## 6. Risk Assessment

### Bull Case

1. **Global naval spending super-cycle:** Rising geopolitical tensions (China-Taiwan, Russia-NATO, Middle East) drive defense budgets higher
2. **KDX-III / KSS-III programs:** Domestic submarine and destroyer programs provide steady revenue through 2035+
3. **Export pipeline:** Potential submarine and warship export contracts with Canada, Poland, Australia, and Middle Eastern nations worth ₩10–20T each
4. **Commercial shipbuilding tailwind:** High vessel rates and green shipping regulations support newbuilding demand

### Bear Case

1. **Korean won exchange rate risk:** Won depreciation would compress USD-denominated margins on exports
2. **Geopolitical reversal:** If geopolitical tensions de-escalate, the defense premium could compress
3. **Execution risk:** Large, complex naval construction programs frequently face delays and cost overruns
4. **Valuation at 52-week highs:** The stock has already rallied 1,108% over 200 weeks — mean reversion is a real risk
5. **No massive volume surge:** Zanger's favorite signal (2x+ volume) is absent — the current setup lacks his "tell"
