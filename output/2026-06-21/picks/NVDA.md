## 1. Methodology Overview — CAN SLIM

William O'Neil's CAN SLIM is a seven-factor framework for identifying institutional-quality growth stocks before they make their largest price advances. The acronym stands for:

| Letter | Factor | Description |
|--------|--------|-------------|
| **C** | Current earnings | EPS must be accelerating — up >25% YoY in the most recent quarter |
| **A** | Annual earnings | Consistent annual EPS growth (>25% per year over 3 years) |
| **N** | New product/service | A novel catalyst that creates competitive advantage |
| **S** | Supply & demand | Low share count or significant buyback program |
| **L** | Leader or laggard | The stock must be an RS leader in its industry group |
| **I** | Institutional sponsorship | Growing ownership by mutual funds, pension funds, banks |
| **M** | Market direction | The general market must be in a confirmed uptrend |

O'Neil famously wrote: *"The big money is not in the buying and selling, but in the waiting."* — *How to Make Money in Stocks* ([source](https://www.amazon.com/How-Make-Money-Stocks-Winning/dp/0071614136))

## 2. Business Overview

NVIDIA Corporation is the dominant designer of graphics processing units (GPUs) and accelerated computing platforms. The company's products span:

- **Data Center & AI:** Hopper, Blackwell, and Rubin GPU architectures for AI training/inference
- **Gaming:** GeForce RTX GPU lineup
- **Professional Visualization:** Quadro and RTX for enterprise graphics
- **Automotive:** DRIVE platform for autonomous vehicles
- **Omniverse:** 3D simulation and digital twin platform

NVIDIA commands an estimated 80–90% market share in AI accelerator silicon, making it the single most important beneficiary of the global artificial intelligence infrastructure buildout.

## 6. Risk Assessment

### Bull Case

1. **Blackwell Ultra demand:** Unmet demand continues through 2027
2. **AI CapEx cycle:** Hyperscalers (Microsoft, Amazon, Google, Meta) continue raising AI infrastructure budgets
3. **Rubin architecture:** Next-gen product cycle ensures competitive moat through 2028+
4. **Software ecosystem:** CUDA lock-in — developers trained on CUDA do not easily switch to competitors

### Bear Case

1. **Competition:** AMD MI400 and custom ASICs (Google TPU, Amazon Trainium, Microsoft Maia) erode market share
2. **Customer concentration:** Top 4 hyperscalers represent >50% of Data Center revenue
3. **Geopolitical risk:** Further export restrictions to China could impact revenue by $5–10B annually
4. **Valuation compression:** At $210.69, NVDA trades at ~32x trailing EPS and ~28x forward EPS — not cheap by historical standards but below its 5-year average P/E of 45x
