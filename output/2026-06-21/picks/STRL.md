## 1. Methodology Overview — SEPA / VCP

Mark Minervini's SEPA (Specific Entry Point Analysis) methodology is a structured approach to identifying explosive growth stocks at precisely the right entry point. The core concept is the **Volatility Contraction Pattern (VCP)** — a tightening of the daily/weekly trading ranges (contracting volatility) on declining volume, which precedes a breakout.

Minervini's key criteria:

1. **Superior earnings:** EPS must be accelerating (recent quarters > prior quarters)
2. **Strong relative strength:** Stock must be among the top performers in the market
3. **VCP pattern:** At least 2–3 contractions in daily/weekly range with declining volume
4. **Rising moving averages:** Price must be above rising 50-day and 200-day MAs
5. **Industry group strength:** Stock must be in a leading sector
6. **Low debt / strong balance sheet:** Financial stability to support growth
7. **Positive accumulation:** Volume on up days > volume on down days
8. **Proprietary entry point:** Precise buy point based on pattern completion

Minervini writes:

> *"I look for stocks in confirmed uptrends that are in the process of forming a tight consolidation."* — Mark Minervini, *Trade Like a Stock Market Wizard* ([source](https://www.amazon.com/Trade-Like-Stock-Market-Wizard/dp/0071807171))

## 6. Risk Assessment

### Bull Case

1. **Data center construction super-cycle:** Hyperscaler CapEx projected to exceed $300B combined in 2027 — STRL is a direct beneficiary
2. **Margin expansion:** As backlog converts, margins should continue expanding as fixed costs are spread over higher revenue
3. **Backlog visibility:** Multi-year backlog provides revenue visibility through 2028+
4. **EPS acceleration trajectory:** If EPS growth continues at >100% YoY, the current P/E will look cheap in 12 months

### Bear Case

1. **Valuation risk:** At 77x trailing earnings, any EPS disappointment will trigger significant multiple compression
2. **Data center CapEx slowdown:** If hyperscalers reduce AI infrastructure spending (recession, oversupply, or shift to inference-only), STRL's growth could slow sharply
3. **Competition:** Turner Construction, DPR Construction, and others compete for data center contracts — margin pressure
4. **Labor shortage:** The construction industry faces structural labor shortages, which could cap growth and expand costs
5. **Commodity cost inflation:** Steel, concrete, and copper price increases could compress margins
