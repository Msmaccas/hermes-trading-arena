## 1. Methodology Overview — Anchored VWAP & Trend Structure

Brian Shannon, author of *Technical Analysis Using Multiple Timeframes*, teaches a systematic approach that aligns multiple timeframe trends with anchored VWAP (Volume-Weighted Average Price) to identify low-risk, high-probability entries. His approach can be summarized in three principles:

1. **Multiple Timeframe Alignment:** The weekly, daily, and intraday trends must all be headed in the same direction (bullish alignment for longs)
2. **VWAP as Dynamic Support/Resistance:** VWAP (especially anchored to significant swing points) acts as a gravitational price center — pullbacks to VWAP on declining volume are low-risk entry points
3. **Volume Confirmation:** Price must be supported by volume; divergences between price and volume are early warning signals

Shannon emphasizes:

> *"When price pulls back to the VWAP or a key moving average on declining volume, that's a low-risk entry."* — Brian Shannon, *Technical Analysis Using Multiple Timeframes* ([source](https://www.amazon.com/Technical-Analysis-Using-Multiple-Timeframes/dp/0615183503))

## 2. Business Overview

Qualcomm is a world leader in wireless technology, semiconductor design, and mobile computing. The company operates three primary segments:

- **QCT (Qualcomm CDMA Technologies):** Chipset design and sales — Snapdragon processors for mobile, automotive, IoT, and edge computing
- **QTL (Qualcomm Technology Licensing):** Patent licensing for CDMA, WCDMA, LTE, 5G, and 6G essential patents
- **Automotive:** Snapdragon Digital Chassis — in-vehicle infotainment, ADAS, and connectivity solutions

With a market capitalization of **~$238B** and a **P/E of ~24.3x**, QCOM represents a more reasonably valued play on AI-at-the-edge compared to the hyperscaler AI trade.

## 6. Risk Assessment

### Bull Case

1. **AI-at-the-edge narrative:** As AI inference shifts to devices, Qualcomm is uniquely positioned with Snapdragon AI engines in billions of devices
2. **Automotive inflection:** $45B design-win pipeline converting to revenue — automotive could become 20%+ of revenue by 2028
3. **PC market disruption:** Snapdragon X Elite could capture 10–20% of the PC market within 3 years
4. **Reasonable valuation:** At 24.3x trailing P/E, QCOM is significantly cheaper than NVIDIA (32x) or PLTR (144x)

### Bear Case

1. **Licensing risk:** QTL revenue is tied to legal settlements and patent licensing agreements — disputes with Apple, Huawei, or others could disrupt this high-margin revenue stream
2. **Smartphone cyclicality:** 60%+ of revenue is tied to the smartphone market, which is mature and cyclical
3. **Competition:** MediaTek, Samsung, and Apple's in-house chip designs all compete with Qualcomm
4. **China exposure:** ~50% of revenue from China — geopolitical tensions or trade restrictions could significantly impact
5. **Valuation already reflects growth:** At ~$238B market cap, QCOM is not "cheap" in absolute terms

### Risk Quantification

| Factor | Probability | Impact |
|--------|-------------|--------|
| Licensing dispute | 20% | HIGH |
| Smartphone demand slowdown | 35% | MODERATE |
| China trade restrictions | 25% | HIGH |
| Automotive pipeline conversion | 40% | POSITIVE |
