## 1. Methodology Overview — Zanger's 10 Keys

Dan Zanger is one of the most successful traders in history, famously turning $10,775 into $18 million in two years (1998–2000) by identifying high-momentum stocks with massive volume surges. His methodology is documented in his newsletter (Zanger's TradersLog) and his YouTube interviews.

Zanger's "10 Keys to Massive Gains" include:

1. **Big volume:** *"I want to see volume — massive volume — coming into the stock"*
2. **Strong price action:** New highs or breaking out of basing patterns
3. **RSI momentum:** RSI above 50 and trending higher, ideally between 60–80
4. **Industry group strength:** The stock's sector must be a leading group
5. **Rising moving averages:** EMAs (20, 50, 200) sloping upward
6. **Tight consolidations:** Prior to the breakout, the stock should form a tight base
7. **Relative strength:** Stock outperforming the broader market
8. **Earnings growth:** Must be present (but secondary to technicals)
9. **Institutional accumulation:** Big money buying evidenced by volume
10. **Risk management:** Defined stops, scaling out of positions

## 2. Business Overview

Centerra Gold Inc. (CGAU) is a Canadian-based gold mining company with operations in North America and select international jurisdictions. Key assets:

- **Mount Milligan Mine (British Columbia):** Copper-gold mine — long-life, low-cost operation
- **Öksüt Mine (Turkey):** High-grade, open-pit gold mine
- **Gold Market Exposure:** Pure-play gold producer benefiting from the gold bull market

As of June 2026, gold is trading at **~$3,400+ per ounce** — at or near all-time highs. Gold miners (junior through senior) have traditionally lagged the metal during the early phase of a gold bull market, then "catch up" with explosive moves once gold stabilizes at higher levels.
