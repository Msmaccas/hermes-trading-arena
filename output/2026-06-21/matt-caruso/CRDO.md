The MACD histogram is negative (-0.17). The -DI is above the +DI. These suggest that momentum may be slowing. If the stock can't follow through on today's breakout, we could see a failed breakout pattern that leads to significant downside.

### Risk 4: Sector Rotation

Technology stocks are sensitive to interest rate expectations and economic data. If the macro environment shifts — higher rates, weaker growth, geopolitical concerns — high-beta tech stocks like CRDO will be sold first and hardest.

### Risk 5: The Volume Question

Volume ratio of 1.14 is decent but not overwhelming. For a breakout to a new all-time high, I'd like to see volume at least 50% above average. 1.14x is barely above average, which suggests institutional conviction might be lacking.
