This is a mid-cap stock. I prefer to trade mid-caps and large-caps because they have liquidity and institutional sponsorship. RDDT has that. But $33.68B is a large base to grow from. **My fundamental verdict**: The EPS growth is real and impressive, but I need to see **revenue acceleration** and **sustainable growth drivers** before I can be fully confident. The P/E of 50 is high, but not a deal-breaker if growth continues.
