# Audience Profiles — High-Ticket Investors

## Primary Audience: High-Net-Worth Individual Investors

**Net worth**: $1M-$50M+
**Portfolio size**: $500K-$20M investable
**Time horizon**: 3-15 years
**Risk tolerance**: Moderate to aggressive
**Knowledge level**: Self-directed investors with 5-30 years experience
**Pain points**: Information overload, conflicting advice, need for conviction before committing capital

### What They Need
- **Contrast, not consensus**: They don't want 10 analysts agreeing. They want 10 different frameworks applied to the same stock so they can see ALL the angles
- **Depth over speed**: They don't need breaking news. They need institutional-quality analysis they can build a thesis around
- **Bear cases**: They've been burned by "this time is different." Every analysis must show them exactly what could go wrong
- **Position sizing guidance**: Not "buy this stock" but "here's how much a $5M portfolio should allocate if this setup works"
- **Global optionality**: They already have US large-cap exposure. They need HK, JP, IN, BR, EU names they haven't considered

### Communication Style Preferences
- Direct, no fluff, no hype
- Data-rich with clear methodology
- Respects their intelligence — no oversimplification
- Admits uncertainty clearly

## Secondary Audience: Institutional Traders

**Firm size**: $50M-$5B AUM
**Role**: Portfolio managers, analysts, prop traders
**Time horizon**: Weeks to quarters
**Knowledge level**: Professional — CFA, advanced degrees
**Constraints**: Mandate-specific (long-only, long/short, sector-specific)

### What They Need
- **Rigorous methodology**: They need to present your analysis to their investment committee. It must hold up to scrutiny
- **Specific price levels**: Entry, stop, target — not ranges
- **Market regime context**: Is this a position for a trending market, a range-bound market, or a crisis?
- **Correlation awareness**: How does this position interact with their existing book?
- **Catalyst timeline**: What specifically needs to happen, and by when?

---

## Tertiary Audience: Trading Community

**Platform**: Twitter/X, TradingView, Discord, Reddit
**Demographic**: Retail traders, students, semi-professionals
**Knowledge level**: Beginner to intermediate
**Time horizon**: Days to months

### What They Need
- **Education, not picks**: Teach methodology through analysis. Show the WORK
- **Verifiable track record**: Link to past calls, show results
- **Repeatable frameworks**: Not "I like this stock" but "here's why this stock fits my system"
- **Risk-first messaging**: Emphasize what can go wrong before what can go right

---

## Tone Guidelines Per Audience

| Aspect | HNW Investor | Institutional | Trading Community |
|--------|-------------|---------------|-------------------|
| Depth | Full report | Full report + data export | Condensed, actionable |
| Jargon | Explain once | Use freely | Explain with links |
| Speed | Prior to open | Pre-market | Real-time |
| Conviction | Probabilistic | Thesis-structured | Entertaining but accurate |
| Bear case | Required | Required | Optional but encouraged |
