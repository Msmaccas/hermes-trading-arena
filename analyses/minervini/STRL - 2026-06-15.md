**The Shakeout:** Sometimes the stock will undercut the prior low of the base. This shakes out the weak holders. But it must rip back within days. If it undercuts and then sits there, that's distribution. If it undercuts and immediately bounces back, that's a shakeout — and it's actually bullish.
