## Micro-Cap Risks

ALK.L is a micro-cap, and micro-caps have specific risks:

1. **Liquidity risk**: You might not be able to exit at your desired price. A 1000 share sell order could move the price 2-3%.
2. **Information asymmetry**: Micro-caps have less analyst coverage. The person buying might know more than you.
3. **Gap risk**: The stock could gap down 20% or more overnight on bad news.
4. **Volatility**: Daily moves of 10-20% are normal for micro-caps.
5. **Bid-ask spreads**: The spread between bid and ask can be 3-5% or more.
6. **Index exclusion**: Micro-caps are not in major indices, so there's no passive buying support.

These risks mean that even a perfect episodic pivot setup can lose money if the liquidity dries up during the exit.
