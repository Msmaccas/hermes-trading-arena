## The Leverage Decay Problem

This is the most important concept for trading CRW3.L:

**Example**: 
- Day 1: CRWV goes from $100 to $110 (+10%)
- CRW3.L goes from 100p to 130p (+30%)
- Day 2: CRWV goes from $110 back to $100 (-9.09%)
- CRW3.L goes from 130p to 94.5p (-27.27%)
- After 2 days: CRWV is flat. CRW3.L is down 5.5%.

**This is leverage decay**. The product only tracks 3x on a daily basis. Over multiple days, the compounding works against you. A volatile but flat market will slowly destroy the NAV of a leveraged ETP.

**What this means for trading**:
1. CRW3.L is a DAYS-LONG trade, not a weeks-long trade
2. If the move stalls, exit. Don't hold and hope.
3. The 43.27% move might be the entire trade in one day
4. If you miss the entry on the gap-up, the R:R is poor on day 2
