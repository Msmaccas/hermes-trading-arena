## The Coeur Mining Story — More Than Just Silver

Coeur Mining operates six mines across North America:
- **Palmarejo** (Mexico): Complex gold-silver operation, high-grade
- **Rochester** (Nevada): Recently expanded, now a heap leach behemoth
- **Kensington** (Alaska): High-grade underground gold mine
- **Wharf** (South Dakota): Open-pit heap leach gold operation
- **Silvertip** (British Columbia): High-grade silver-zinc-lead operation
- **Las Chispas** (Mexico): Recently acquired, high-grade silver

The catalyst for the operational improvement:
1. **Rochester Expansion**: The build-out of the Rochester mine was completed in 2024-2025, doubling throughput. This was a multi-year, capital-intensive project that weighed on financials during construction.
2. **Las Chispas Integration**: The acquisition of the Las Chispas mine added high-grade silver production.
3. **Cost Control**: The company has driven all-in sustaining costs lower while increasing production.

The result: Coeur Mining has been added to the S&P MidCap 400 index. Analyst consensus is Strong Buy, with a price target of $22.40-$27.50. Scotiabank recently resumed coverage with an Outperform rating and $27.50 price target.
