## The 40x Volume Phenomenon — What It Really Means

Let me explain what 40x volume actually means in practical terms:

1. **Normal daily volume**: ~315K shares (3-month average)
2. **Pivot day volume**: ~12.6M shares (40x normal)
3. **Outstanding shares**: ~21M
4. **Float traded**: 60% of the entire float turned over in one day

Sixty percent of the company changed hands in a single session. That is an extraordinary number. When 60% of a company's stock trades in one day, it means the entire shareholder base is turning over. Old holders are selling to new holders.

**The question**: Who is selling and who is buying?

If old holders (long-term investors who bought at the IPO or lower) are selling, that's distribution — bad.
If short sellers are covering, that's a squeeze — neutral to bullish in the short term.
If new institutional buyers are accumulating, that's accumulation — bullish.

The 40x volume with a 65% up-move suggests short covering or new accumulation. Distribution would happen on down-volume.
