## ANCHORED VWAP ANALYSIS — THE CORE OF MY METHOD

The VWAP is the true dollar average price for the period studied. Each share traded (by retail and institutional traders, long and short) receives equal weight in the calculation. (https://alphatrends.net/anchored-vwap/)

The VWAP is more responsive to volume trends than price action because of the volume weighting. Accordingly, as volume levels change throughout the day (the pace of trade), heavier volume periods have more impact on the movement of the VWAP, while lighter volume periods have less impact. (https://alphatrends.net/anchored-vwap/)

The VWAP is a straightforward study. There are no settings, adjustments, or offsets to complicate its measurement. (https://alphatrends.net/anchored-vwap/)

When we start the VWAP calculation at a point other than the start of the current day it becomes 'anchored' to that first point and we call it an 'AVWAP.' (https://alphatrends.net/anchored-vwap/)

The AVWAP broadcasts the message of the market more clearly than any other technical tool I have studied in my 30+ year obsession with the stock market. (https://alphatrends.net/anchored-vwap/)

The 'anchor point' is the most subjective part of our analysis. (https://alphatrends.net/anchored-vwap/)

We can measure AVWAP on any security or market where price and volume data are available. That includes individual stocks, ETFs, futures contracts, cryptocurrencies, and Forex markets. (https://alphatrends.net/anchored-vwap/)
