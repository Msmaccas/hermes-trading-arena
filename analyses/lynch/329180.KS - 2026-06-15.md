**COMPANY OVERVIEW & BUSINESS MODEL**

HD Hyundai is the largest shipbuilding company in the world. They build everything from LNG carriers to container ships to naval vessels. The company was formerly known as Hyundai Heavy Industries, and it’s been around since 1972. They have the largest shipyard in the world in Ulsan, South Korea.

Here’s what I love about this business: it’s a tollbooth. Every time someone wants to move goods across an ocean, they need a ship. And when ships get old—typically 20-25 years—they need to be replaced. The global fleet is aging, and new environmental regulations are forcing shipping companies to buy more efficient vessels. That’s a multi-year tailwind.

The competitive position is strong. HD Hyundai has pricing power because they’re the low-cost producer with the most advanced technology. They’re building the next generation of LNG carriers, which are in huge demand because Europe needs to replace Russian gas. They’re also building methanol-ready container ships for the world’s largest shipping lines.

The company has a 20-year order backlog worth over 60 trillion won. That’s visibility I love. When I buy a stock, I want to know where the earnings are coming from. With HD Hyundai, I can see the revenue for the next 3-5 years. That’s rare in most industries.

The business model is simple: win orders, build ships, deliver them, collect payment. The key metric to watch is the order book. As long as orders keep coming in, earnings will keep growing. And right now, orders are coming in faster than they can build them.
