Now, the key question for any cyclical is: **Where are we in the cycle?** Are we at the peak, the trough, or somewhere in between? Because if you buy a cyclical at the peak, you're going to get killed. If you buy at the trough, you can make a fortune.

Let me give you an example. In the early 1980s, Chrysler was trading at $2 a share. Everyone thought they were going bankrupt. Lee Iacocca came in, got government loans, turned the company around.
