American is stuck in the middle. They are not the cheapest. They are not the best. They are not the most premium.

But the airline itself is a commodity. You fly American, Delta, or United. They are all the same to most passengers.

**I prefer businesses with a moat.** Coca-Cola has a moat. McDonald’s has a moat. AAL does not have a strong moat.
