### THE PEG RATIO: WHY IT DOESN'T APPLY HERE (AND WHY THAT'S DANGEROUS)

But here’s the problem with AMC. The P/E is -14.6. You cannot calculate a PEG ratio for a company losing money. A negative P/E divided by a positive growth rate gives you a negative number. A negative PEG ratio is meaningless.
