CONSULTING BLUEPRINT — HARD RULES
- Niche: Pick a "crying pain" in a profitable market. Industry must have money flowing (e.g., dentists, real estate agents, law firms). If there's no money in the industry, there's no money for you.
- Offer: One specific transformation. Not "I help businesses grow." Must be: "I help [niche] get [specific result] in [timeframe]." Vagueness kills sales.
- Lead Gen: Cold outreach only to start. DM, email, direct mail. No ads until you've closed 10+ clients manually.
- Close: Diagnostic call only — never a pitch call. You diagnose, you don't sell.
- Deliver: Fixed scope, fixed timeline, fixed price. Package the delivery so it's repeatable — every client gets the same system.
- Scale: Systemize each role → hire for each role → remove yourself from delivery. If the business stops when you stop working, you don't have a business — you have a job.

SYSTEMIZE — HARD RULES
1. DOCUMENT: Record every process step-by-step. Loom videos, SOP docs, checklists. If it's not documented, it doesn't exist.
2. DELEGATE: Hire a VA or specialist to execute the documented process. Test them with the SOP. Your first hire should be a virtual assistant, not a COO.
3. DISAPPEAR: Remove yourself entirely from the operation. Check KPIs only. The goal is to work ON the business, not IN the business.
4. Systemize before you scale. Scaling broken systems = scaling chaos.
5. One system at a time. Do not try to systemize everything at once — pick the bottleneck first.

DIAGNOSTIC CALL — STRICT STRUCTURE
1. Discovery (first 10 min): Ask. Do not tell. "What's happening? How long? What have you tried? What's it costing you?" Gather data only.
2. Diagnosis (next 10 min): Identify the root cause. Show them exactly what's broken. "Here's the real problem you didn't even know you had."
3. Prescription (next 10 min): Present your solution. Specific steps. Timeline. Expected outcome.
4. Price (next 5 min): State the price flatly. No hesitation. No discounts. No packages to confuse.
5. Close (remaining time): Handle objections with facts, not flattery.

HIGH-TICKET PRICING — HARD RULES
- Price based on VALUE of the transformation, NOT hours of work.
- Minimum ticket: $2,000-$3,000/month. Below that = not enough margin for fulfillment and overhead.
- Never negotiate price. If they ask for a discount, they don't value the outcome enough.
- Three pricing tiers: Good ($2-3k/mo), Better ($5-7k/mo), Best ($10k+/mo). Same system, different access levels.
- Retainer model only. One-off projects die. You want recurring revenue.
- Raise prices every 3 months if you're full. If you're turning people away, your price is too low.

CLIENT FULFILLMENT — HARD RULES
- Onboarding call within 48 hours of payment. Momentum is everything.
- Deliver the first result within the first 14 days. If they don't see progress in two weeks, they'll cancel in four.
- Weekly check-in calls for first 30 days, then bi-weekly.
- Results documentation: Case studies from every client (even if small).
- Referral ask: End of month 2, ask for referrals.

AGENCY OPS — HARD RULES
- First hire: Virtual assistant for admin ($500-800/mo). Frees up 20+ hours/week.
- Second hire: Delivery specialist (someone who can execute your system without you).
- Third hire: Sales closer (commission-only to start).
- Track three numbers daily: leads in, calls booked, clients closed.
- Weekly team meeting: 15 minutes max. No status updates.
- Client dashboard: Track deliverables, milestones, satisfaction score.
