## THREE CORE SETUPS

### 1. Continuation Breakout (Flags / VCPs / Cup & Handle / Darvas Boxes / Wedges)
- Stock in established uptrend (10 EMA > 20 EMA > 50 SMA)
- Consolidation lasts 2 weeks to 2 months
- Volatility contracts (range tightens left to right)
- Volume dries up during consolidation
- Higher lows as base builds
- Stock surfs the 10 or 21 EMA during consolidation
- Entry: Breakout above the consolidation high on opening range breakout (ORB)
- **"Stocks move like stairs — leg higher, sideways or pullback, leg higher, sideways, leg higher."**

### 2. Episodic Pivot (Gap-Up Base Breakout)
- Gap up on strong earnings / catalyst (beat EPS + revenue, guide higher)
- Bases for several days to weeks above the gap
- Breakout from that base = continuation of the new uptrend
- Often starts the entire multi-month trend
- **Scan criteria:** Stock gaps up 3%+ on earnings, volume > 150% average, consolidates above gap with tight range
- Fundamental catalyst provides conviction — bigger position size, hold longer
- **"If you can identify these groups... you will have a big edge versus trading a random stock."**
- **"Fundamentals can give you conviction. If I took fundamentals out of my trading, my profitability would drop a lot."**

### 3. Parabolic Short / Mean Reversion
- Stock up 200–1000%+ in a short time (3–5 sessions to 2 weeks)
- Must wait — do NOT short day 1 or day 2
- Wait for day 4, 5, or later when the move is truly extended
- Entry: Opening range lows fail, VWAP fails, first red candle on 5m/60m
- Stop: Above the day's high or recent swing high
- Mean reversion long is the mirror image (down 60%+ in few days, first green candle = entry)
- **"You wait for weakness — you don't start randomly picking tops."**
- **"The ones that go up for three, four, even five days — those are the best ones."**

## ENTRY RULES

1. **Daily chart must show a clean breakout setup** — obvious consolidation with tightening range, higher lows, drying volume
2. **Buy on intraday time frame using ORB (Opening Range Breakout):**
   - **1m ORH** (aggressive): First 1-minute candle high — highest failure rate but earliest entry
   - **5m ORH** (balanced): First 5-minute candle high — preferred for most trades
   - **60m ORH** (safe): First 60-minute candle high — lower failure rate, later entry
3. **Enter all at once** — no scaling in. "I buy everything at once, very aggressively."
4. **Buy the breakout, not the anticipation** — "I don't anticipate breakouts, I buy them as they break."
5. **Stock must be in the top 2% of momentum** — screen on 1m, 6m, 12m, 18m time frames
6. **Volume confirmation preferred** but less important for liquid large caps
7. **Fundamental catalyst = higher conviction** — earnings beats, revenue growth, theme/group strength
8. **Post-correction plays** — look for stocks that held up best during the market decline. "Almost like free money."

### First Pullback Entry (Alternate)
- If you missed the breakout, wait for the first pullback to the 5 EMA or 10 EMA
- Enter as stock bounces off the EMA with an ORH trigger
- Stop below the pullback low

### Fibonacci Targets (after entry)
- **1.618** extension = first partial profit zone (sell 1/3 to 1/2)
- **2.618** extension = hold with trailing stop
- **4.236** extension = home run territory

## EXIT RULES

### Initial Stop
- **Low of the day (ORL)** of the entry candle time frame
- Should not be wider than 1x ATR / ADR
- If stop exceeds ATR, position size is too large or setup is wrong

### Partial Profit Taking
- After 3–5 days of momentum burst, sell 1/3 to 1/2 of the position
- "Sell some into that first burst to lock in a little bit of profits... now it's a stress-free trade."
- Worst case: break-even or small gain on the remainder

### Trailing Stop (Remainder)
- Use 10 EMA (fast stocks) or 20 EMA (slower stocks)
- **Wait for daily close below the MA** — intraday violations are common shakeouts
- "Three quarters of the time it doesn't pay to override your sell rules."

### Climax Close Exit
- If a stock closes near its low after a huge extended run (200%+ in days), sell the remainder
- Especially if volume is massive and the candle is a big red engulfing

### 20% Tightening Rule
- If a stock in profit tightens its daily range by 20%+ vs the prior 5-day average range (volatility contraction at the top), ring the register — big move may be exhausting

### 100%+ Trail Rule
- Once a position is up 100%+, tighten the trailing stop to the 10 EMA
- Protect the bulk of the gain — do not give back a double

### Ahead of Catalysts
- If holding into earnings / FDA decision / presentation with no profit padding, sell entire position
- "I don't like to gamble over earnings. If I don't have a big profit padding, I just sell it."

### Max Loss Per Trade
- **Hard stop at 1R loss.** If the setup breaks, get out. Do not move stops lower.
- "You have to get used to getting stopped out a lot."

### Max Daily Loss
- **-3% of account = stop trading for the day.** Shut it down. Come back tomorrow.
- This is non-negotiable. The best traders know when to walk away.

## POSITION SIZING (Kelly-Optimal Framework)

### Core Parameters
- **Win rate:** 25–35% (use 30% for calculation)
- **Avg win:** 3:1 R or better (3R on winners)
- **Avg loss:** 1R (controlled with stop)
- **Kelly fraction:** f* = (0.30 × 3 − 0.70) / 3 = (0.90 − 0.70) / 3 = 0.20 / 3 ≈ **6.7%** of account per bet (full Kelly)
- **Practical (half-Kelly):** ~3.3%, but Qullamaggie operates with **15–25% per position** because his stops are very tight (often <1–2% of stock price), making the dollar risk per trade still ~1–2% of account

### Dollar Risk Math
- If stop is 2% below entry and position is 20% of account → risk = 0.20 × 2% = **0.4% of account**
- If stop is 5% below entry and position is 20% of account → risk = 0.20 × 5% = **1.0% of account**
- **Target: 0.5–1.5% total account risk per trade**

### Scaling
- **At 10 positions:** ~10% total account at risk (full deployment)
- **At 20–30 positions:** Diversified across different setups and sectors
- **Warning at 30 positions:** History shows multi-week drawdown typically follows
- **Account growth:** When account doubles, risk/size doubles with a lag of a few months
- **Cash during downturns:** Full cash is fine. "You don't need to trade all the time."

## SCANNING METHODOLOGY

### Nightly Scans (TC2000 / MarketSmith)
- **Volume filter:** Minimum $60M daily dollar volume
- **ADR filter:** Average daily range >= 2.4%
- **Momentum filter:** Top 2–7% strongest stocks on 1m, 3m, 6m, 12m, 18m time frames
- **5-day gainer scan:** Stocks up 25%+ in 5 days with $60M volume
- **Result:** ~40–50 stocks for nightly review

### Pattern Recognition (Manual)
- Cannot scan for patterns — you must train your eye
- Build an Evernote database with thousands of before/after screenshots (daily + intraday)
- Study 500–1,000+ hours per setup
- "I have breakouts from 1980... tens of thousands of stocks here. You have to train your brain."

### What Makes a 5-Star Setup
1. Prior uptrend is smooth and clean (10 EMA respected)
2. Consolidation has higher lows
3. Range tightens from left to right (VCP)
4. Volume dries up during the base
5. Stock surfs the 10 or 21 EMA
6. Trigger bar is tight (small range, low volume)
7. Breakout day has volume confirmation
