## Core Methodology — 3 Setups Only

### Setup 1: Episodic Pivot (THE primary pattern — 80%+ of returns)

**What it is:** A stock that has been quiet/moving sideways for weeks or months suddenly gaps up 10%+ on massive volume and HOLDS the gain.

**Entry Criteria:**
- Gap up of **10-40%** (ideally 15-25%) on **3-5x average volume**
- Stock must HOLD the gap — close in the upper half of the day's range
- Prior period: Quiet consolidation for **3+ weeks** (at least) with normal volume
- The stock should close near the high of the day
- Enter at **market open next day** if stock holds overnight

**Why it works:**
- The gap represents institutional buying urgency
- "When a stock gaps 10% on 5x volume and holds, the big money is telling you something"
- These are typically catalysts: earnings beat, new contract, regulatory approval, product launch

> *"Episodic pivots are the most powerful pattern. When a stock gaps 10%+ on massive volume and holds, that's the big money telling you something."*
> — Kristjan Qullamaggie, Chat With Traders (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vqH7IdfjIIw)

### Setup 2: Breakout from Consolidation

**What it is:** Stock breaks above a multi-week or multi-month consolidation range on increased volume.

**Entry Criteria:**
- Base duration: 3-16 weeks (3-7 weeks preferred)
- Breakout on volume > 1.5x average
- Base is relatively tight (not wide-ranging days)
- The stock is already in an uptrend (higher highs, higher lows on the weekly)
- Enter at breakout high + a few cents for confirmation

### Setup 3: Parabolic Short (RARELY — only when all conditions align)

**What it is:** A stock that has gone straight up on declining volume near a climax top.

**Entry Criteria:**
- Stock has gone up 100-300%+ in 3-6 months
- Volume is drying up on the latest push
- Climax: a final 15-20% spike on huge volume (the "thrust") then stalls
- Short at the first failure to make a new high
- Stop: above the recent high

## The 4 Phases of a Trade

1. **Accumulation** — Quiet base building, low volume, tight range
2. **Markup** — The breakout, the move up, the gains. **Phase of maximum profit**
3. **Distribution** — Volume increases but price stalls. Smart money selling
4. **Markdown** — The decline. Don't be here

## Entry Rules

### Episodic Pivot Entry
1. Scan daily for stocks gapping 8%+ pre-market
2. Filter by volume — MUST see 3x+ average
3. Check the chart: Was the stock consolidating for 3+ weeks?
4. Enter at **open next day** if stock held overnight
5. **ORB entry**: If stock continues strong in first 15-30 minutes, add to position

### The ORB (Opening Range Breakout)
- The high of the first 15 minutes is a KEY LEVEL
- If stock breaks above ORB high on volume, that's another confirmation
- Enter additional position at ORB break
- Set first stop just below ORB low

### Position Sizing
- **15-25% per position** (the Kelly formula says 25% optimal given 50%+ win rate and 3:1 R)
- Maximum 4-5 positions at any time
- **NEVER risk more than 3% of account on a single day**
- If you lose 3% in a day, STOP TRADING

## Exit Rules

### Profit Taking (The Hard Part)
- **First target**: 1.618 Fibonacci extension of initial move
- **Second target**: 2.618 Fibonacci extension
- **Third target**: 4.236 Fibonacci extension (the "big one")
- Trail stop at **20-day EMA** (tightening) or **50-day EMA** (wider)
- **Sell 1/3 at 20%+ profit, let the rest run**
- *"If a stock doesn't work within a few days, it's probably not going to work"*

### Climax Top Recognition
- Final parabolic move on massive volume
- The stock goes up 20-30% in 2-3 days
- Volume is the HIGHEST in months
- Close is weak (near low of day)
- **Sell everything at the close of the climax day**

### Stop Loss
- **3-5% from entry** for high-beta positions
- **7-8% from entry** for normal positions
- If stopped out, wait for the NEXT setup — don't chase
- **The hardest thing is to have the discipline to stay small when you're not seeing good setups**

## The Fibonacci Extension Framework

For any impulsive move, compute extensions from the base:
- **1.618**: First profit target. Take partial profits here
- **2.618**: Strong move. Tighten stops aggressively
- **4.236**: Extinction-level move. This is where multi-baggers happen
