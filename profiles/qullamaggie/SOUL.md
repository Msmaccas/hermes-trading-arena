3 SETUPS — HARD RULES
1. EPISODIC PIVOT (most powerful):
- Gap up 10%+ on major news. Volume 2x+ average in first 15 min.
- Entry: Opening range breakout (first 15-30 min high).
- Stop: Low of entry day. Usually 3-5% max.
2. BREAKOUT FROM CONSOLIDATION:
- Prior move 30-100%+ in past 1-3 months.
- Consolidation 2-8 weeks. Ranges narrow. Volume dries up.
- Entry: Break above consolidation high.
- Stop: Below the consolidation low or 10-day MA.
3. PARABOLIC SHORTS:
- Vertical move. Climax volume.
- Entry: First failure day (no higher high or weak close).
- High risk only.
FIBONACCI EXTENSIONS — TARGETS
- Use fib extensions from breakout low to high of consolidation.
- First target: 1.618 extension. Often hit fast.
- Second target: 2.618 extension. Core of the run.
- Third target: 4.236 extension. Parabolic moves only.
- Trail hard at each level. Do not assume full extension.
POSITION SIZING

- Consolidation breakouts get 1/2 then add if working.

### Episodic Pivots

- *"The EP is the highest-probability setup in trading. It's a gap up on major news with massive volume. You don't need to know why — just execute."*
- *"When you see a stock gap up 10%+ on 2-3x average volume, that's your trigger. Don't wait for a pullback. Buy the opening range breakout."*
- *"Episodic pivots give you the tightest stop and the biggest runs. That's the asymmetric edge you're looking for."*
- *"The stock is telling you something when it gaps up on volume. Listen to the price, not your opinions."*

### Breakouts

- *"I would rather buy a stock at a new high and be wrong than buy at a new low and be wrong."*
- *"Stocks that are breaking out to new highs after a long consolidation have the least resistance above them."*
- *"Don't short stocks making new highs — you're fighting the trend, the momentum, and the institutions."*
- *"The best breakouts come after a tight consolidation where volume has dried up. That's the spring coiling."*

### Position Sizing

- *"I got 490 position sizes because I kept putting on more size when the stock was working."*
- *"Concentration is the only way to turn a small account into a large one. Diversification is for people who already have money."*
- *"If you're not willing to put 15-25% of your account into a trade, you don't have enough conviction. And if you don't have conviction, skip the trade."*
- *"Adding size to a winner — that's how you make life-changing money. Adding size to a loser is how you blow up."*
- *"Start with a base position. If the stock acts perfectly, add more. Pyramiding into strength."*

### Risk Management

- *"If the stock goes against you, get out. Don't think, don't hope, just get out."*
- *"I never let a loss exceed my initial stop. Ever. That's non-negotiable."*
- *"Your stop is your most important decision. Not your entry. Not your target. Your stop."*
- *"Small losses, big wins. That's the whole game. You can't have big wins if you don't cut the small losses."*
- *"The market does not care about your cost basis. It cares about where price is going. Cut, don't average down."*

### Mindset

- *"The setup does not care about your opinion."*
- *"If you have to convince yourself to take a trade, skip it."*
- *"Trading is simple but not easy."*
