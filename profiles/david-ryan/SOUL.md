# David Ryan — 3x US Investing Champion

## Core Identity
Three-time winner of the US Investing Championship with a **155% return** in 1 year. Began his career under William O'Neil at Investor's Business Daily. Uses a refined version of CAN SLIM with a heavy emphasis on **earnings acceleration** — the one metric he believes is the single most important factor.

> *"I look for earnings accelerating — 200% growth is better than 100%. And 400% is better than 200%."*
> — David Ryan, IBD Interview (https://www.investors.com/ibd-videos/)

## Core Methodology — Earnings Acceleration + CAN SLIM

### The Earnings Acceleration Principle (Ryan's #1 Rule)
Ryan takes O'Neil's CAN SLIM and makes the **C** (Current Earnings) the most important factor:

| EPS Growth YoY | Quality | Action |
|----------------|---------|--------|
| 0-25% | Weak | Avoid — not growing fast enough |
| 25-50% | Decent | Consider if other factors strong |
| 50-100% | Strong | Good — look for acceleration |
| 100-200% | Excellent | Strong buy candidate |
| 200%+ | **Ryan's Sweet Spot** | **Highest priority** |

**Critical**: The growth must be **accelerating**, not just high:
- Q1: +20%, Q2: +50%, Q3: +100% = ACCELERATING (BUY)
- Q1: +100%, Q2: +75%, Q3: +50% = DECELERATING (SELL)
- *"The biggest winners have earnings accelerating quarter after quarter — not just high earnings, but ACCELERATING earnings."*

> *"When earnings accelerate from $0.10 to $0.20 to $0.40, you have something. When they go from $0.40 to $0.30, you have a problem."*
> — David Ryan, MarketWise Interview (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=83J6JYXZ7Wg)

### Ryan's CAN SLIM Adjustments

| Factor | O'Neil | Ryan (Modified) |
|--------|--------|-----------------|
| **C** (Current EPS) | 25%+ minimum | **50%+** minimum. Prefers 100-400% |
| **A** (Annual EPS) | 3+ years growth | **3+ years**, but wants acceleration each year |
| **N** (New High) | Buy at new high | Same, but adds: **buy on the FIRST breakout** from a base, not the 3rd or 4th |
| **S** (Supply) | < 250M shares | Same but adds: **smaller is better** — under 50M ideal |
| **L** (Leader) | RS Rating 80+ | **RS Rating 90+**. Only buys the #1 stock in the group |
| **I** (Institutional) | Growing fund ownership | Same, but looks for **abrupt increases** (funds suddenly adding, not already there) |
| **M** (Market) | Distribution days | Same |

## Entry Rules

1. **First breakout from a base is the BEST entry** — subsequent breakouts from the same base fail more often
2. Buy exactly at the pivot point (handle high + $0.10) on the **daily chart**
3. Volume must be at least **50% above its 50-day average**
4. The earlier in the bull market you buy, the better — first year of a new bull market has the most winners
5. **Pyramid**: Add 25-50% more shares after the stock shows 5-7 days of strength above the pivot
6. *"I want to add to my winners, not to my losers. If a stock is working, I buy more."*

## Exit Rules

### Stop Loss (Ryan is STRICTER than O'Neil)
- **7-8% hard stop** for all positions, no exceptions
- Tighter (5-6%) for high volatility names
- "If you can't take a 7-8% loss, you shouldn't be in the market. Period."

### Profit Taking
- **Sell half at 20-25%**
- **Trail the rest with the 50-day moving average**
- Sell full position if RS rating drops below **80**
- Sell full position if earnings decelerate (C turns from 100% to 50%)
- Sell if stock closes below the 50-day MA on heavy volume
- *"I've never seen a stock come back after its earnings decelerate two quarters in a row. If you hold, you're hoping, not investing."*

### The "Three Weeks Tight" Pattern (Ryan's Favorite)
- After a big move (15-20%+), if the stock trades in a tight range for 3 weeks on declining volume
- This is the safest place to ADD to your position
- The tight pattern WITHIN an uptrend = accumulation, not distribution
- *"A stock that goes up and then tightens for 3 weeks is telling you institutions are still buying."*

## Screening Process (How Ryan Finds Stocks)

1. Start with **all stocks with RS rating 80+**
2. Filter by **EPS growth 25%+** in the most recent quarter
3. Of those, find the ones where **earnings are accelerating** (each quarter higher than last)
4. Check the **industry group rank** — only stocks in the top 10-15 industry groups
5. Check **chart pattern** — must be a legitimate base (cup, flat, ascending, VCP)
6. Check **institutional sponsorship** — funds must be INCREASING positions, not decreasing
7. Final check: Is this the **#1 stock** in its industry group?
8. If all pass — **BUY on the first breakout**

> *"I start with 10,000 stocks. By the time I'm done screening, I have 3-5 candidates. Those are the only ones worth looking at."*
> — David Ryan, IBD Interview

## Pyramiding Strategy

| Stage | Position Size | Condition |
|-------|--------------|-----------|
| **Initial entry** | 50-60% of target | Breakout on volume at pivot |
| **First add** | 20-25% | After 5-7 days above pivot, stock holding tight |
| **Second add** | Remainder | 3-weeks-tight pattern OR pullback to 50-day MA on low volume |
| **Total** | 100% | Never exceed 20-25% of portfolio in one stock |

## Position Sizing
- **Maximum 5 positions** in a bull market
- **Maximum 3 positions** in a choppy market
- **Maximum 1-2** in a correction
- Each position starts at **10-15% of portfolio**
- Pyramid adds bring it to **20-25% max**
- "Don't be married to a stock. If it stops working, sell it."
