HOOKS — HARD RULES
- First 3 seconds decide everything. If you haven't created tension by second 3, you've already lost them.
- A hook must create a LOOP in the viewer's brain — an open question their mind NEEDS to close. "Your hook isn't a line, it's a neurological trap."
- The Curiosity Gap formula: open a gap between what they know and what they want to know. "Tell them what they DON'T know about something they ALREADY care about."
- Pattern Interrupt: first line must shatter what they expected to hear. "If they can predict your next word, they're gone."
- No informational hooks (boring). No emotional hooks (manipulative). Use SUGGESTION hooks — make them feel like THEY discovered the value.
- Specificity beats hype. "I lost 10k followers in 3 days" beats "I had a bad week."
- Format rule: Hook → Context → Payoff. Never open with the payoff. The payoff is the REWARD for staying through the tension.

STORYTELLING — THE NEUROSCIENCE FRAMEWORK
- "The Neuroscience of Addictive Storytelling" — stories work because they trigger dopamine, oxytocin, and cortisol in sequence.
- Structure every story: Tension (cortisol) → Connection (oxytocin) → Resolution (dopamine). Miss one = flat story.
- The Power of Suggestion: don't tell them the lesson. Plant the idea so THEY connect the dots. "When someone figures it out themselves, they OWN it."
- Use the "But/Therefore" method, not "And then." Each beat of the story must either oppose (but) or cause (therefore) the next. "And then" is a lullaby. "But" is a hook.
- Specific sensory details trigger mirror neurons. "Don't say 'I was nervous.' Say 'my hands were shaking so bad I could barely tap the screen.'"
- Stories without stakes are anecdotes. Every story needs: What I wanted → What blocked me → What I did → What happened.
- "The biggest mistake? Making yourself the hero. Make the AUDIENCE the hero. You're just the guide."

PERSONAL BRANDING — MAGNETIC AS F*CK
- "The NEW Era of Personal Brands is Here" — the old model was "look at me, I'm successful." The new model is "here's a worldview that makes you see the world differently."
- Your brand IS your POV. Not your niche, not your bio. "If you don't have a controversial take, you don't have a brand."
- The Magnetism Formula: Authority (what you know) + Vulnerability (what you've been through) + Worldview (how you see the game) = Magnetic.
- Relatability without authority = friend. Authority without relatability = guru. Both = someone they'd PAY to hear from.
- "Regular People Making Millions" — the barrier isn't credentials, it's willingness to say something that makes people uncomfortable.
- Personal brand ≠ content machine. "If your brand would still exist without you, it's not a personal brand. It's a media company."
- Post your failures publicly. "People don't trust the highlight reel. They trust the behind-the-scenes where everything went wrong."

THE ALGORITHM — HOW IT ACTUALLY WORKS
- "How Social Media Algorithms Actually Work (And How to Beat Them)" — algorithms optimize for one thing: session time. If you keep people ON the platform, you win.
- The algorithm doesn't care about your content quality. It cares about COMPLETION RATE and REPEAT VIEWERS. "One person watching 10 times beats 10 people watching once."
- Shares > Likes > Comments > Saves, in that order. "Shares are the only metric that matters because shares bring NEW viewers into the ecosystem."
- The Retention Graph is your report card. "If your retention graph drops in the first 3 seconds, no amount of good content after that matters."
- Watch time is a function of pacing, not length. "A 60-second video that holds 80% retention beats a 30-second video that holds 50%."
- Algorithm honeymoon: new accounts get boosted to test engagement. "The algorithm doesn't know who you are yet, so it shows you to everyone. Use this window."
- "The BIGGEST Lies You've Been Told About Social Media" — consistency doesn't beat quality. Daily posting garbage won't outrank weekly posting gold. "The algorithm rewards the video, not the schedule."

CONTENT SYSTEMS — THE SHORTFORM SYSTEM
- "Copy This System, It'll Blow Up Your Social Media" — a system beats motivation every time. "You shouldn't wake up wondering what to post."
- The 3-Bucket System: Hooks (tested in a swipe file) → Scripts (templated frameworks) → Production (batch-recorded).
- Batch creation rule: record 10 videos in one session. "I don't write scripts day-of. I write 20 scripts in one sitting, then record all of them."
- The Swipe File is your most valuable asset. Save every hook that made you stop scrolling. "Don't create from scratch. Remix what already works."
- Repurpose rule: one long-form piece of content → 10 short-form pieces. "You don't need more ideas. You need more angles on the same idea."
- Use Claude/AI for hook iteration: give it your best hook, tell it to generate 20 variations. "The first idea is rarely the best idea. The 17th variation is where the magic happens."
- "Speaking Like This Gives You an Unfair Advantage" — pacing matters. Short sentences. Pauses. Emphasis on the last word of the sentence. "Your voice is a delivery mechanism. If the delivery is flat, the message doesn't land."

BUILDING AN OBSESSED AUDIENCE
- "How to Build an Audience That's Obsessed With You" — obsession comes from SUGGESTION, not sales. "You can't tell someone to be obsessed. You have to make them feel like they discovered you."
- The Identity Loop: attract people who want to BE you → give them a framework to become you → they bond over the shared transformation. "Your audience isn't people who like your content. Your audience is people who want your identity."
- Micro-community over macro-audience. "10,000 people who would pay you $100 is better than 100,000 people who wouldn't pay you a dollar."
- Engagement bait is a trap. "If you have to trick people into commenting, you've already lost. Real engagement comes from real resonance."
- Comment sections are goldmines. "Every comment is a free content brief. Someone literally told you what they want to hear about."
- The Power of Suggestion applied to audience building: don't say "I'm an expert." Create content that makes them think "this person knows what they're talking about." The conclusion should be THEIRS.

SHORT FORM VIDEO — SPECIFIC RULES
- Vertical video only. 9:16 aspect ratio. "If you're still filming horizontal for social, you're fighting the platform."
- Text on screen in the first frame. "Your hook should work on mute. Add captions that emphasize the hook."
- Cuts every 3-5 seconds minimum. "Dead air kills retention. If there's a pause longer than 1 second, cut it."
- Eye contact: look at the camera lens, not the screen. "Looking at yourself breaks the illusion of connection."
- Lighting rule: key light in front, not above. "Ring light at eye level. If you have shadows on your face, you look like you're hiding something."
- Hook → Setup → Body → CTA in under 60 seconds for best retention.
- Call to action: never "like and subscribe." Use "follow for more [specific thing]." "If you say 'like and subscribe,' I guarantee you half your audience doesn't know what they're subscribing FOR."
- "The NEW Way to WIN on Social Media in 2026" — AI thumbnails, AI-generated hooks from viral templates, and letting the algorithm find your audience instead of chasing trends.
