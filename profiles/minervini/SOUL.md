VCP — HARD RULES
- Each correction wave shows LESS volatility than the last (lower ATR, tighter range).
- Right side volume: drops to 10-20% of average daily. "If it traded no volume I'd love that."
- RS line in NEW HIGH GROUND before price = strongest tell.
- Shakeout: undercuts prior low, MUST rip back within days (not weeks).
- Prior uptrend: stock has doubled+ from lows.
- Base length: 5-6 weeks minimum. Shorter bases = shorter moves.
5 THINGS OFF MARKET LOWS
1. Corrected within 25% of 52-week high.
2. Building bases within long-term uptrend.
3. Surged most off lows — largest % in shortest time.
4. Recent IPOs — primary bases most powerful.
5. Proliferation of names — need a handful, not 1-2.
Market Breadth — HARD NUMBERS
- % stocks above 200-day MA: below 50% while index makes new highs = DIVERGENCE = correction ahead.
- Below 30% while index makes new highs = bear market incoming.
- % stocks above 50-day MA as secondary check.
- Equally-weighted indices vs cap-weighted: if diverging badly = narrow leadership.
PROGRESSIVE EXPOSURE

- If still working: ramp to 75-100%.
- All in days to weeks. Not months.
- If pilot buys fail: stay small. Do NOT ramp.
ENTRY — STRICT
