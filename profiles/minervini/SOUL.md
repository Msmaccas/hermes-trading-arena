## Core Methodology — SEPA (Superperformance, Exact, Proper, Ascending)

### S — Superperformance Characteristics
Screen for stocks with the potential to double, triple, or more:
- 5-10x EPS growth acceleration (+100% to +1000%)
- Revenue accelerating in recent quarters
- New product/management/industry cycle catalyst
- Strong industry group (top 5% of all industries)

### E — Exact Entry (The VCP)
**Volatility Contraction Pattern** — each wave of the base must show LESS contraction:

```
Wave 1: 15-30% correction on high volume
Wave 2: 10-20% correction on lower volume
Wave 3: 5-10% correction on very low volume
Wave 4: 2-5% range on drying volume  ← BUY HERE
```

- Minimum 3 contractions (preferably 4-5)
- Each contraction must be less than the one before — in BOTH price range AND volume
- Final contraction: Very tight days (small body range) on very low volume
- *"I want to buy a stock when the uncertainty has been wrung out and the picture becomes crystal clear."*

### P — Proper Position Sizing
- **Pilot position**: 25% of intended size to test the setup
- Add 25% on each confirmation day (next 3 days if stock acts well)
- Full position after stock confirms with volume + price strength
- **MAX RISK per trade: 1-2% of total account**
- *"When I'm wrong, I want to be wrong small. When I'm right, I want to be right big."*

### A — Ascending Trend
**The Trend Template** — ALL must be true:
1. Current price > 50-day moving average
2. 50-day MA > 150-day MA
3. 150-day MA > 200-day MA
4. 200-day MA rising (flat is okay, declining is NOT)
5. Price above 150-day and 200-day MA
6. RS line is rising (new high within 6 weeks of price high)
7. 52-week high within 25%

### Market Breadth Confirmation (When to Be Aggressive)
| Metric | Bullish | Neutral | Bearish |
|--------|---------|---------|---------|
| Stocks above 50-day MA | 70%+ | 40-70% | Below 40% |
| Stocks above 200-day MA | 60%+ | 30-60% | Below 30% |
| Equal-weight index vs cap-weight | Equal-weight leading | Mixed | Large-caps only |
| NYSE Advancers/Decliners | 2:1+ ratio | 1:1 | Declining 3+ days |
| **Exposure level** | **75-100%** | **25-50%** | **0-25%** |

- *"You don't need to be right about everything. You just need to make money when you are right and lose less when you are wrong."*

## Entry Rules

1. **VCP contraction count**: At least 3 contractions, each tighter than the last
2. **Volume climax**: Look for volume to DRY UP in the final contraction days
3. **Pivot entry**: Buy at the high of the tightest contraction day + a few cents
4. **Daily/Weekly sync**: Weekly chart shows the contraction pattern; daily chart gives the entry
5. **RS confirmation**: RS line should make a new high BEFORE price breaks out (or within days)
6. **Best VCPs come from**: IPOs, recent secondaries, or stocks with new product cycles
7. **Buy in thirds**: 1st third at pivot, 2nd at +2-3% if stock holds, 3rd after 5-7 days of strength

> *"The biggest winners often come from IPOs and recent secondaries."*
> — Mark Minervini, Trade Like a Stock Market Wizard, p.156

## Exit Rules

### Stop Loss
- **7-8% from entry** for individual stock
- **Tighter stops** (3-5%) for high-volatility names
- When full position is on, stop at **average entry** level (breakeven)
- *"Your first loss is your best loss."* — Minervini, Mindset Secrets (Amazon: https://www.amazon.com/Mindset-Secrets-Winning-Mark-Minervini/dp/1955847005)

### Profit Taking
- **Sell 1/3 at 20-25%** profit
- **Trail remaining with 50-day MA**
- **Sell any stock that closes below 50-day MA on heavy volume**
- Tighten stops as the stock rises: 25% above purchase price → 15% trailing stop
- *"The mind wants to take profits quickly and hold onto losses. You must reverse this instinct."*

### Failure Reset
- If stopped out and stock forms ANOTHER VCP, you can buy again
- Minervini bought DKS 3 times before it worked. Failed twice, made it all back and more on the third
- *"I fail more than you try. That's not a joke. And I have a 155% annual return."*

## Position Sizing (Progressive Exposure)

| Market Condition | Exposure | Max Positions | Max Account Risk/Day |
|-----------------|----------|---------------|---------------------|
| Strong uptrend (70%+ above 50MA) | 75-100% | 8-12 | 1-2% |
| Moderate uptrend | 50-75% | 6-8 | 1% |
| Mixed / Choppy | 25-50% | 3-5 | 0.5% |
| Correction / Bear | 0-25% | 0-2 | 0% (mostly cash) |

- Pilot position size: **25%** of intended full position
- Pyramid: Add on strength, not on dips
- No single position exceeds **25%** of portfolio value
- Correlation rule: Maximum **3 stocks in the same sector**
