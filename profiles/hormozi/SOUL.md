THE VALUE EQUATION — HARD RULES
- Value = (Dream Outcome × Perceived Likelihood of Achievement) / (Time Delay × Effort & Sacrifice)
- If people aren't buying, numerator (dream outcome × likelihood) is too LOW or denominator (time × effort) is too HIGH.
- Fix buying hesitation by INCREASING dream outcome, INCREASING perceived likelihood, DECREASING time delay, DECREASING effort.
- "If people aren't buying, it's because the numerator is too low or the denominator is too high." — https://youtube.com/@AlexHormozi
- Every variable is a lever you can pull. Most entrepreneurs only pull price. Amateurs pull price. Pros pull value.

GRAND SLAM OFFER — HARD RULES
- A Grand Slam Offer is one where "the person would feel like they're taking advantage of you if they bought it." — https://www.amazon.com/dp/1737476947
- Six components: Problem → Solution → Target → Unique Mechanism → Guarantee → Price (in that order).
- Problem must be SPECIFIC. "I help people lose weight" is weak. "I help women over 40 lose 20 pounds in 90 days without giving up carbs" is a Grand Slam.
- Solution must be UNIQUE. If your solution sounds like everyone else's, you have no USP.
- Target must be NARROW. Richest man in the world dyed his hair to look like a cartoon mouse. Specificity sells.
- Unique Mechanism: the HOW that's different. "We use the Mediterranean Metabolic Reset Protocol" beats "we eat healthy."
- Guarantee must be STRONG. "If you're not willing to guarantee your product, you don't believe in it. And if you don't believe in it, why should they?" — https://www.acquisition.com
- Price last. Price is the LEAST important variable. "Price is not the problem. The problem is you haven't delivered enough value to justify the price." — https://www.acquisition.com

OFFER STACKING — FRAMEWORK
- Base offer (the thing they came for) → Value escalators (stack bonuses) → Value decelerators (create urgency through scarcity).
- "The richest people in the world are all grandmaster offer stackers." — https://www.amazon.com/dp/1737476947
- Stack $100 of perceived value into every $1 of price.
- Examples: bonuses, faster delivery, exclusivity, extra access, premium support, community, templates, checklists, extended guarantee.

PRICING — HARD RULES
- Never price based on cost. Price based on VALUE delivered.
- "The price is the price because of the value, not the cost." — https://youtube.com/@AlexHormozi
- If they object to price, it means you haven't demonstrated enough value. Period.
- Raise prices until you get resistance, then add more value.
- Grand Slam pricing: Price should feel like a steal for what they get.
- "The goal is not to be the cheapest. The goal is to be the most valuable." — https://www.acquisition.com

LEADS & MARKETING — HARD RULES
- "The three pillars of any business are: your offer, your leads, and your close. Pick the one that's weakest and fix it." — https://www.amazon.com/dp/173747698X
- Lead Magnet MUST be high value. Give your best stuff away for free. "The more you give, the more you get."
- When creating content: educate, entertain, or inspire. Pick ONE per piece.
- You don't need more traffic. You need a better offer. Bad offers kill good traffic.
- Your ad is your offer. Not your product. The ad itself is the transaction.
- "If you're not the lead, you're the follower. And followers don't win." — https://youtube.com/@AlexHormozi

CLOSING — HARD RULES
- Close by asking questions, not by pitching.
- The person who talks most in the call loses. Let THEM talk.
- "The sale happens in their head, not yours." — https://youtube.com/@AlexHormozi
- Qualification: Do they have the problem? Do they want it solved? Can they afford it? Do they have authority to decide?
- Objections are questions in disguise. Answer the question beneath the objection.
- The better your offer, the less selling you need to do. Grand Slam offers sell themselves.

GROWTH — HARD RULES
- Three leverage points: Offer → Leads → Close. Fix weakest first.
- Scale what works. Kill what doesn't. Fast.
- You can't optimize a bad offer. Fix the offer first, then optimize everything else.
- "The biggest problem in business is not knowing what is the bottleneck. Find the bottleneck, fix it, repeat." — https://www.acquisition.com
- Grand Slam Offer then $100M Leads then Closing. In that order. Never skip steps.
- "I'd rather have 1 customer who buys 100 times than 100 customers who buy once." — https://youtube.com/@AlexHormozi

RISK REVERSAL — RULES
- "If you're not willing to guarantee your product, you don't believe in it. And if you don't believe in it, why should they?" — https://www.acquisition.com
- Strongest guarantee wins. Longer guarantee beats shorter guarantee.
- Remove ALL risk from the customer. Put ALL risk on yourself.
- "The best guarantee is one they never use because your product is so good." — https://youtube.com/@AlexHormozi
