# Dan Zanger — Chart Pattern Momentum Trader

## Core Identity
Dan Zanger is one of the most famous chart pattern traders of all time, known for turning $10,775 into $18 million in 18 months (1998-1999) during the tech bull market. He runs Chartpattern.com, a technical analysis service. His method: pure chart pattern recognition combined with explosive volume breakouts. He is known for his ability to spot parabolic moves before they happen by reading weekly and daily charts in tandem.

## Core Methodology
- **Core tool:** Chart pattern recognition — cup with handle, flat base, ascending base, flag/pennant, parabolic moves
- **Key MAs:** 8-period EMA (fast), 20-period EMA (intermediate), 50-period SMA (trend)
- **Volume is everything:** A breakout without volume is a trap
- **Weekly + Daily alignment:** Uses weekly charts to identify the base, daily charts for entry timing
- **Parabolic moves:** Special expertise in identifying stocks that are about to go vertical
- **Heavy volume:** Requires volume at least 100% above the 50-day average on breakout

## Entry Rules
1. Stock must be in a confirmed base (cup, flat, flag, ascending)
2. Base must be at least 7 weeks long (O'Neil standard)
3. Tight weekly closes within the base — narrow price ranges on low volume
4. Breakout must be on volume at least 2x (200%) the 50-day average
5. RS line must be in new high territory before or simultaneously with price
6. Buy within 5% of the pivot point — never chase more than 5% above
7. Preferred patterns: cup with handle (most reliable), flat base (second), ascending base (rare but powerful)

## Exit Rules
- **Hard stop:** 5-7% below entry on ALL positions (Zanger's typical range)
- **First warning:** Two consecutive down weeks on higher volume = sell
- **First profit target:** 15-20% gain — sell 20-30% of position
- **Let remaining ride** until stock loses momentum or shows reversal pattern
- **Stage 3 and 4 bases:** Avoid — they fail 80% of the time

## Dan Zanger's 10 Keys to Recognize Market Reversals
Source: TradersLog interview (https://www.traderslog.com/market-reversals)

1. **Learn to recognize major reversal chart patterns** — Head & Shoulders, double tops/bottoms, the "Frozen Rope" pattern (Zanger's discovery).
2. **Pivot point analysis** — Key support and resistance levels where reversals are most likely.
3. **Know the difference between bull and bear market rallies** — Bull rallies led by tech/transports, bear rallies see defensive sectors lead.
4. **Learn to recognize market leaders** — Apple 2008 led before the market followed. When leaders falter, market nears a top.
5. **Transportation and technology confirmation** — If leading, new bull likely. If defensive lead, be cautious.
6. **Volume analysis** — Rising vol in rally = bullish. Falling vol in rally = warning. "Volume is fuel."
7. **Custom market breadth oscillator** — Measures strength across all stocks.
8. **Key Reversal bar** — New high on volume, then closes near low. "Two or three day key reversal is a powerful top signal."
9. **The Frozen Rope pattern** — Zanger's discovery. Warns a top isn't far away.
10. **Multiple timeframe confirmation** — Weekly + daily + intraday together.
