## METHODOLOGY: THE CAN SLIM® SYSTEM

Every letter is a hard filter — skip one and you're speculating, not investing.

### C = CURRENT QUARTERLY EARNINGS — THE HIGHER, THE BETTER

**Hard rules:**
- Minimum: EPS up 18-20% from same quarter last year (p.163)
- Ideal: 40-100-200%+ accelerating growth
- **"The percentage change in EPS is the single most important element in stock selection today."** (p.161)
- Sales growth must also be rising — accelerating revenues confirm earnings
- **"Strong and improving quarterly earnings should always be supported by sales growth of at least 25% for the latest quarter."** (p.165)
- Check for accelerating growth over the last 3 quarters
- Avoid extraordinary/non-recurring gains
- Two quarters of major deceleration = trouble

### A = ANNUAL EARNINGS — SIGNIFICANT GROWTH

**Hard rules:**
- Annual EPS growth of 25%+ in each of the last 3 years (p.168)
- Each year should show accelerating growth
- ROE minimum 17%, ideally 25-50% (p.169)
- Pre-tax profit margin must be strong
- Cash flow per share at least 20% greater than EPS
- Stability rating (1-99, lower = more stable)

### N = NEW PRODUCTS, NEW MANAGEMENT, NEW HIGHS

**Hard rules:**
- New product, new service, new management, or significant new industry change
- Stock must be buying at new price highs — NOT at lows
- Buy at the right time — breakout from a proper base
- **"As a smart investor, your job is to buy when a stock looks too high to the majority of conventional investors and sell after it moves substantially higher."** (p.183)

### S = SUPPLY AND DEMAND — BIG VOLUME DEMAND AT KEY POINTS

**Hard rules:**
- Smaller float preferred (fewer shares outstanding)
- Heavy volume on up weeks = institutional demand
- Volume at breakout MUST be 50%+ above average, ideally 200-500-1000%+ (p.124)
- Volume dry-ups near lows of base are constructive
- Stock buybacks are positive

### L = LEADER OR LAGGARD

**Hard rules:**
- RS Rating minimum 80 (top 20% of all stocks) (p.196)
- Average RS of best performers was 87
- #1 or #2 in its industry group — NEVER buy #3+
- Composite Rating 90+ in bull markets
- Buy only the leading stock in a leading industry group

### I = INSTITUTIONAL SPONSORSHIP

**Hard rules:**
- At least several institutional owners
- At least 1-2 mutual fund owners with top-notch recent performance
- Increasing sponsorship in recent quarters
- Management ownership is a plus
- **"Institutional trading can account for up to 70% of the activity in the stocks of most leading companies."** (p.201)

### M = MARKET DIRECTION — HOW TO DETERMINE IT

**Hard rules:**
- Check distribution days FIRST. 5-6 in 4-5 weeks = market topping
- Distribution day = index closes DOWN on HIGHER volume
- NEVER buy in a correction — wait for a follow-through day
- **"Four out of five stocks will go down with the general market, so you must know which way the wind is blowing."** (Chapter 9)
- **"Never fight the market — it's bigger than you are."** (p.243)

**Follow-Through Day — The Green Light:**
- DO NOT buy on day 1, 2, or 3 of an attempted rally
- Follow-through: on 4th day+, index closes up 1%+ on HIGHER volume than previous day
- Most powerful follow-throughs occur on days 4-7
- **"No new bull market has ever started without a strong price and volume follow-through confirmation."** (p.228)

## CHART PATTERNS — HARD RULES

### Cup-with-Handle (Primary Pattern — 90% of all bases)

**Characteristics:**
- Correction (cup): 15-35% ideally (can go 50-70% in severe bears)
- Duration: 7-65 weeks (most 3-6 months)
- Handle: drifts DOWN 10-15% on lighter volume, 3-5+ weeks minimum
- Handle must form in the UPPER HALF of the base
- Tight closes in handle — very narrow price range, volume dry-up
- "U" bottom (not narrow "V")
- Pivot point: handle's high + $0.10 above (buy at exactly this price)
- Breakout volume: MUST be 50%+ above average, ideally 100-200%+
- RS line in NEW HIGH territory BEFORE or simultaneously with price
- Prior uptrend of at least 30% on strong volume

**DO NOT BUY:**
- Handles that wedge up along lows (failure-prone)
- Handles forming in lower half of base
- Bases that are too wide/loose/deep (>50% correction in bull markets)
- Third or fourth-stage bases (higher failure rates)
- Stocks more than 5-10% past the correct pivot point

### Other Patterns:

| Pattern | Price Range | Duration | Key Traits |
|---------|-------------|----------|-----------|
| Flat Base | 10-15% | 5+ weeks | Tight closes, sideways drift |
| Ascending Base | Three pullbacks | 5+ weeks | Each pullback has a higher low |
| High Tight Flag | 10-25% correction | 3-5 weeks | Stock doubles in 4-8 weeks prior |
| Double Bottom | "W" shape | 7-65 weeks | Second low tests first low |
| Sauce-with-Handle | Shallow | Extended timeframe | Longer, shallower cup |

## BUY RULES — STRICT

1. **Buy EXACTLY at the pivot point** (handle high + $0.10). Never chase 5-10% above.
2. **Buy on breakout day** with volume 50%+ above average
3. **First follow-up buy** at +2-2.5% above purchase price (pyramiding UP)
4. **Never buy more than 5% past the pivot point**
5. **If stock surges 20% in 1-3 weeks**, hold minimum 8 weeks
6. **First pullback to 10-week MA is NORMAL** — hold through it
7. **RS line must confirm** — at new high territory or concurrently with price
8. **The best time to buy a stock during any bull market is when the stock initially emerges from a price consolidation or sound 'basing' area of at least seven or eight weeks."** (p.311)

## SELL RULES — STRICT

### Cutting Losses (The Defense)

1. **Cut loss at 7-8% MAXIMUM.** Hard stop. No exceptions. (p.249)
2. **"The whole secret to winning big in the stock market is not to be right all the time, but to lose the least amount possible when you're wrong."** (p.247)
3. Sell without hesitation — no waiting, no hoping, no need for market close
4. In bear markets, tighten to 3% stop loss
5. NEVER average down (buying more of a losing stock)
6. **"Take your losses quickly and your profits slowly."** (p.254)

### Taking Profits (The Offense)

1. Sell 3 out of 4 stocks at **20-25% gain** (p.265-266)
2. The 1 in 4 true leaders — hold for the big move (100%+)
3. If a stock gains 20% in less than 3 weeks, hold for **8 weeks minimum** (p.265)
4. For true leaders, hold for long-term capital gains (6-12 months)
5. Sell laggards and force-feed into winners

### Climax Top Warning Signs (Sell Signals)

- Largest daily price run-up since the start of the advance
- Heaviest daily volume on the top day
- Exhaustion gap — opens on a gap up from previous day's close
- After a long advance, two weeks down in a row on higher volume = sell immediately
- Peak closes on huge volume
- "Railroad tracks" — two weeks retracing same area with heavy volume, no further price progress
- Breaking an upper channel line

### Pyramiding Rules (from Livermore, p.264):
1. Initial purchase: at exact pivot point
2. First add: at +2% to 2.5% from initial cost
3. Second add: smaller increment after further confirmation
4. Rule: **"If you pyramid, keep your average cost low by never buying more shares at the higher prices... This is averaging up rather than averaging down."** (The Limited chart, p.70)
