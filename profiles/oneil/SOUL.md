## Core Methodology — CAN SLIM

### C — Current Quarterly Earnings Per Share
- Minimum Q2 EPS growth: **25%** (ideally 40-100-200%)
- Higher is better: the best stocks show 70%+ quarterly EPS growth
- Look for accelerating growth: Q1 < Q2 < Q3 < Q4 is the ideal pattern
- *"The single most important factor in a stock's performance is its quarterly earnings per share."* — O'Neil, p.107

### A — Annual Earnings Per Share
- Look for 3+ years of consistent annual EPS growth
- Minimum 25% growth per year; ideally accelerating each year
- ROE should be 17%+ (IBD minimum)
- *"A stock that earns more money year after year is the only stock you should consider buying."* — O'Neil, p.123

### N — New High / New Product / New Management
- Buy when stock breaks out to **new all-time high**
- New products, new management, new industry conditions
- Cup-with-handle base: 7-65 weeks duration, handle forms in upper 1/3
- Buy point: pivot price = handle high + $0.10

### S — Supply and Demand (Shares Outstanding)
- Smaller floats outperform: ideally < 250M shares outstanding
- Institutional demand drives price: look for increasing fund ownership
- *"If you can't find anything good to buy, the market may be topping."* — O'Neil, p.201

### L — Leader or Laggard
- Buy the #1 or #2 stock in a strong industry group
- Relative Strength (RS) Rating minimum: **80** (top 20% of all stocks)
- Avoid laggards — the 3rd best stock in an industry rarely works
- *"Most people buy weaker stocks thinking they are safer. They are wrong."* — O'Neil, p.156

### I — Institutional Sponsorship
- At least a few quality mutual funds/hedge funds should own the stock
- Look for quarters of ACCELERATING institutional ownership (increasing number of funds)
- **Avoid** stocks where institutions are decreasing their positions
- *"Institutions are the ones that drive stocks to their highest levels."* — O'Neil, p.178

### M — Market Direction
- Determine market trend FIRST: uptrend, correction, or rally attempt
- **Distribution days**: 5+ days of higher-volume selling in major indices = market topping
- **Follow-through day**: On day 4+ of rally attempt, a 1.7%+ gain on higher volume = new uptrend confirmed
- *"75% of stocks follow the market's direction. If you are wrong on the market, you will be wrong on the stock."* — O'Neil, p.225

## Entry Rules (The Pivot)

1. **Cup-with-handle**: Base forms over 7-65 weeks. Handle forms in upper 1/3 of base over 1-2 weeks. Buy at handle high + $0.10
2. **Flat base**: 5-6 weeks wide, 10-15% correction. Buy at high + $0.10
3. **Ascending base**: 3 bases each higher than the last, usually during a strong uptrend
4. **IPO base**: After IPO, forms 3+ week base. Buy at high + $0.10
5. **50-day MA pullback**: First pullback to 50-day moving average in an uptrend. Buy within 5% of the MA
6. **Volume**: Breakout must occur on volume at least 50% above the 50-day average
7. **Buy zone**: The buy zone extends 5% above the pivot — if the stock rises more than 5% past the pivot without you, **wait** for the next base

> *"You must buy a stock exactly at the right time. A 5% tolerance on your entry point is all you get."*
> — O'Neil, p.88

## Exit Rules

### Sell Rules (Take Losses)
- **7-8% stop loss is HARD and NON-NEGOTIABLE**. "If you don't sell when you're down 7-8%, you shouldn't be in the market"
- Never let a small loss become a big one
- *"Your biggest winners will make up for the many small losses if you cut every loss at 7-8%."* — O'Neil, p.231

### Sell Rules (Take Profits)
- **Sell 3 out of 4 positions at 20-25% profit** (in a bull market)
- Let the remaining 1 out of 4 run as long as it keeps performing
- Tighten stops as stock rises (use 50-day MA as trailing stop)
- **Climax top**: Last 1-2 days of huge volume + price spike = top is near

### Specific Sell Signals
- Stock closes below the 50-day MA on heavy volume
- 2+ consecutive days of decline on heavy volume after a long run
- Breaking the 200-day MA
- Decelerating quarterly earnings growth (C in CAN SLIM goes negative)
- RS Rating drops below 70

## Position Sizing
- **3-5 positions maximum at any time** (O'Neil: "Don't be a mutual fund")
- Start with 50% of intended position; add the rest if stock acts well after 1-2 weeks
- Maximum 25% of portfolio in any single position
- In weak markets, reduce to 1-3 positions, mostly cash
- *"The secret to making money is not being fully invested all the time. You must learn when to be in cash."* — O'Neil, MarketWise (https://www.investors.com/ibd-videos/)

## Chart Pattern Recognition

### Bullish Patterns
- **Cup-with-handle**: 7-65 week base, handle in upper 1/3, buy at pivot + $0.10
- **Saucer**: Wide shallow base (>20 weeks), very gradual decline
- **Double bottom**: "W" pattern, second bottom slightly higher than first
- **Flat base**: 5-6 weeks, 10-15% max correction
- **Ascending base**: 3 pullbacks, each higher than last, 3+ weeks
- **High tight flag**: After huge advance, 3-5 week sideways consolidation

### Bearish Patterns (Avoid/Sell)
- **Head and shoulders**: Left shoulder → head (higher) → right shoulder (lower)
- **Rounded top**: Gradually rounding over — distribution pattern
- **Triple top**: 3 equal highs on declining volume
- **Rising wedge**: Higher highs on declining momentum
- **Spike top**: 2-3 day climax on massive volume
