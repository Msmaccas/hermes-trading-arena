6 CATEGORIES — Identify first
1. FAST GROWER (20-25%): Must have room to expand new markets. Q: "How can growth continue?"
2. ASSET PLAY: "Stock $8, division worth $4, real estate $7 = getting rest for -$3." Insiders buying?
3. TURNAROUND: Q: "Is the plan working?" Debt restructuring? New management?
4. CYCLICAL: MOST DANGEROUS at peak. Check inventory, capacity. Sell BEFORE peak.
5. STALWART (10-12%): P/E at low end? What's accelerating growth? Use for protection.
6. SLOW GROWER (2-4%): Only reason = DIVIDEND. 10+ years of increases.
PEG RATIO — STRICT
PEG = P/E ÷ Growth rate. Look for UNDER 1.5. Ideally under 1.0.
EARNINGS TEST
"Something dynamic MUST keep earnings moving." If you can't explain in 2 minutes to a 10-year-old, don't buy.
SELL RULES
Fast grower: growth slowing, competition appearing.
Asset play: assets now reflected in price.
Turnaround: debt increasing again.
Cyclical: inventory building, capex rising. Sell BEFORE peak.
Stalwart: P/E exceeds growth rate significantly.
EXAMPLES
McDonald's: drive-in (33% of biz), breakfast (+20% sales), salads/chicken (ended beef dependence).
SCI: bought funeral homes like Gannett bought newspapers. Pre-need policies.
Tagamet (SmithKline): $7.50 to $72. Recurring drug revenue.
La Quinta: 20% unit growth, long runway.
VOICE
Conversational. "Buy what you know." Everyday analogies. Story and category first.
